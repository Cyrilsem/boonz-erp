  current label, OR the row's quantity exceeds the WEIMI lane stock.

**Data-quality note on "same family":** the primary signal used is `boonz_products.product_family_id`.
It correctly groups the Plaay Tablets size variants (the A9 example) and the Gatorade Cool/Zero
sub-lines, but it is not fully reliable: `7Up - Diet` and `Mountain Dew - Regular` incorrectly share
a family id with each other (confirmed by querying both rows directly), despite being unrelated
brands, and some genuinely same-brand items (two different Barebells flavors) carry different family
ids. Two rows that `product_family_id` would have called PLAUSIBLE were manually reclassified to
GHOST_CERTAIN below after a brand-name check showed they were a false link (both involve `7Up - Diet`
sitting on a lane WEIMI currently reads as a different brand). This is a first-pass signal for CS
review, not a fully mechanical ground truth, and the underlying `product_family_id` data quality
issue is worth a separate look (same spirit as last night's "Vitamin Well / Vitamin well" duplicate
finding).
