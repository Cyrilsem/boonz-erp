# PRD-110 P4.4b — post-facto fill capture at the machine · DARA DESIGN + CODY REVIEW

Authored relay **leg 102** (2026-08-04). Status: **DESIGN COMPLETE, NOT BUILT.** No migration applied.
Every claim below was read out of the live database this leg. The two load-bearing claims were settled
by **executing the adversarial case in a rolled-back subtransaction**, not by reading source — per
S-177 (a constraint that reads correctly can still be off for exactly the rows you care about).

BUILD SPEC P4.4b text: _"Post-facto fill capture at the machine (LIVE incident 07-30 01:21, fixture
26): driver fill screen accepts filled ≠ planned with a **source split** — `n from warehouse + m
spot-bought` (+ optional flavour breakdown, pod-level accepted when SKUs unknown). WH leg debits only
n (guard unchanged — it was right to refuse); spot leg writes a pending spot_fill event + attaches to
the open walk-in PO (or creates one) with qty m; PO receive (same night or morning, with receipt
expiries) completes the chain: WH in-and-out netted or direct-to-machine receive (Dara call), shelf
composition updated to n+m, conservation exact end-to-end. Driver is NEVER stranded on an error
message: the error path becomes a guided 'record it properly' flow."_

## 0. THE INCIDENT, TRACED TO THE EXACT LINE THAT FIRED

Driver, 2026-07-30 01:21: _"I put 9 bcz I buy 8 zigi in carrefour and 1 pc from warehouse."_

`receive_dispatch_line` (207 lines, `prosrc`) does this and only this:

```
v_overfill := GREATEST(p_filled_quantity - v_planned, 0);          -- line 33  → 9 - 1 = 8
... FOR v_fefo IN wh_fefo_for_line(...) LOOP  ... v_need := v_need - v_take; -- lines 93-111 → nothing pickable
IF v_need > 0 THEN                                                  -- line 112
  RAISE EXCEPTION 'receive_dispatch_line: overfill of % unit(s) ... Refusing to
    silently debit an arbitrary/zero row.'                          -- line 113
```

⭐ **The guard was RIGHT and it stays byte-for-byte unchanged.** The 8 units never passed through any
warehouse, so there was no WH row to debit; debiting one anyway is the phantom-mint this codebase has
spent whole PRDs eliminating. ⛔ **P4.4b must never be built by weakening line 112.** The defect is not
the refusal — it is that refusal was the _only_ thing offered. The driver had told us the exact truth
(`1 wh + 8 spot`) and the system had no place to put it.

## 1. THE DARA CALL — ⭐ **DIRECT-TO-MACHINE RECEIVE. NOT WH IN-AND-OUT NETTED.**

The BUILD SPEC left this open. It is now closed on **executed** evidence, not on taste.

### ⛔ The probe that decided it (leg 102, rolled back, zero residue)

Simulating exactly what "WH in-and-out netted" does — INSERT the spot batch into
`warehouse_inventory` at qty 8, then net it straight back out to 0 in the same transaction:

```
proposals_before=1148  proposals_after=1149  status_after_netting=Inactive
```

Residue verified afterwards: `proposals_now=1148`, `probe_wh_rows=0`. Clean.

**Netting therefore does two forbidden things, every single time a driver spot-fills:**

1. ⛔ **It UPDATEs `warehouse_inventory.status` to `Inactive`.** `tg_propose_inactivate_on_zero_stock`
   fires on the drain-to-zero and its body ends in a literal
   `UPDATE public.warehouse_inventory SET status = 'Inactive'`. That column is **Article 6
   manager-only, propose-then-confirm only** — non-negotiable per standing memory rule. The trigger is
   a sanctioned exception for a _genuine_ drain of a real batch; a batch born and killed in one
   transaction for goods that were never at the warehouse is not that.
2. ⛔ **It mints an auto-confirmed `warehouse_inventory_status_proposal` row** (`+1`, status
   `'confirmed'`, `decision_note` "Auto-confirmed"). Every post-facto fill would silt the manager's
   proposal queue with a decision nobody made about a batch that never existed.

⚠️ Recorded in passing, **not this unit's job to fix**: `propose_inactivate_on_zero_stock` calls
`set_config('app.via_rpc','true', true)` and **never restores it** — a ninth GUC-leak site in the
S-160 family. Do not drift into it; log it and move on.

### ⭐ DESIGN DECISION D-E: the spot units never touch `warehouse_inventory`

They went Carrefour → driver's hand → shelf. That is the physical truth and the ledger records it.
Phase 2 closes the **financial** chain on `purchase_orders` alone. There is no warehouse leg because
there was no warehouse.

⚠️ **The residual, stated rather than hidden:** this breaks the "a received PO line has a matching
`warehouse_inventory` batch" shape that the incumbent `receive_purchase_order` always produces.
That shape is an _observed regularity of the WH path, not a declared invariant_ — no CHECK, no view
and no cron asserts it (probed). Fixture 26 pins the divergence deliberately (§5, seq 30-33) so a
later leg reads it as intended, not as a bug to "repair" by adding the phantom batch back.

## 2. WHY THIS IS TWO PHASES, AND WHY THAT IS NOT A COMPROMISE

The BUILD SPEC's own words — _"PO receive (same night **or morning**, with receipt expiries)"_ — make
the two legs **separate transactions**. At 01:21 the units are already on the shelf; the receipt is
processed hours later. So there is a real, correct interval in which:

> the goods are physically in the machine and financially unreceived.

⭐ That is an ordinary accounting state (goods on hand, invoice pending), and the design names it
rather than papering over it. It is also precisely why netting is wrong: by the morning, "receiving
into the warehouse" would be fiction about goods demonstrably sitting on a shelf.

## 3. ⛔ P4.4b CANNOT JUST CALL `create_spot_purchase_v3`

`create_spot_purchase_v3(p_machine_id, p_warehouse_id, p_supplier_id, p_lines, p_dispatch_date,
p_receipt_photo, p_po_id, p_note, p_dry_run)` — verified live, one overload — takes
`p_warehouse_id` **NOT NULL** and its step 4 **receives into that warehouse** (P4.4 design D-A). That
is the correct behaviour for P4.4's case (driver buys stock _for_ the warehouse) and the wrong one for
P4.4b (driver buys stock _already in the machine_).

⭐ **DESIGN DECISION D-F:** P4.4b reuses `create_spot_purchase_v3`'s **PO-resolution** rule — attach to
today's open walk-in PO for that supplier, else mint — but **not** its receive. The shared logic is
lifted into a small helper so the two callers cannot drift apart:
`_resolve_open_walkin_po_v3(p_supplier_id, p_po_id) RETURNS text`. ⛔ Do **not** widen
`create_spot_purchase_v3` with a "skip receive" boolean — a flag that switches off the core of a
protected-entity writer is how that writer stops being reviewable (Article 3).

## 4. SIGNATURES (Dara)

### Phase 1 — at the machine

```sql
public.receive_dispatch_line_sourced_v3(
  p_dispatch_id      uuid,                     -- the line the driver is filling
  p_qty_from_wh      numeric,                  -- n. Debited via the incumbent, unchanged.
  p_qty_spot         numeric,                  -- m. Physically on the shelf, financially pending.
  p_spot_supplier_id uuid    DEFAULT NULL,     -- REQUIRED when m > 0; Active + walk_in
  p_spot_breakdown   jsonb   DEFAULT NULL,     -- optional flavour split; NULL ⇒ pod-level (SKUs unknown)
  p_unit_price_aed   numeric DEFAULT NULL,
  p_receipt_photo    text    DEFAULT NULL,
  p_note             text    DEFAULT NULL,
  p_received_by      uuid    DEFAULT NULL,
  p_dry_run          boolean DEFAULT false
) RETURNS jsonb
```

⭐ **The split is explicit and required — never inferred.** A single `filled_quantity` that exceeds
plan is exactly the ambiguity that stranded the driver; the RPC refuses to guess which units came from
where. `n + m` is the filled quantity; neither is derived from the other.

⭐ **`p_spot_breakdown` is genuinely optional** (BUILD SPEC: _"pod-level accepted when SKUs unknown"_).
The 07-30 driver bought "8 zigi" of mixed flavours he did not enumerate. When NULL, the m units are
recorded against the dispatch line's own `boonz_product_id` at pod level and
`spot_fill_v3.sku_resolved = false` marks them for later refinement. ⛔ Do **not** force a fake SKU
split — an invented flavour breakdown is worse than an honest pod-level record.

### Phase 2 — closing the chain

```sql
public.receive_spot_fill_po_v3(
  p_po_id        text,
  p_lines        jsonb,      -- [{spot_fill_id, expiry_date, price_per_unit_aed}] — receipt expiries
  p_received_by  uuid    DEFAULT NULL,
  p_dry_run      boolean DEFAULT false
) RETURNS jsonb
```

Both **SECURITY DEFINER**, `search_path = public, pg_temp`. Per **S-140/S-178**: `REVOKE ALL FROM
PUBLIC, anon, authenticated`, then `GRANT EXECUTE` to the one intended role set, then **read `proacl`
back and assert the whole string** — ⛔ Supabase's default privileges arm every new `public` object at
birth and a `GRANT` does not reduce an existing ACL. Role gate: `field_staff` and up on phase 1 (a
driver at the machine is the primary caller); warehouse/manager on phase 2.

## 5. NEW TABLE — `spot_fill_v3` (Dara)

The "pending spot_fill event" the BUILD SPEC names. Additive, versioned, non-protected.

```sql
spot_fill_v3(
  spot_fill_id    uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  dispatch_id     uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES refill_dispatching(dispatch_id),
  machine_id      uuid NOT NULL,
  shelf_id        uuid,
  boonz_product_id uuid NOT NULL,
  qty             numeric NOT NULL CHECK (qty > 0),
  sku_resolved    boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false,
  supplier_id     uuid NOT NULL,
  po_id           text,
  status          text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'pending'
                    CHECK (status IN ('pending','received','void')),
  unit_price_aed  numeric,
  expiry_date     date,                        -- NULL until phase 2 supplies the receipt expiry
  receipt_photo   text,
  created_at      timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  received_at     timestamptz,
  created_by      uuid,
  received_by     uuid,
  note            text,
  CONSTRAINT spot_fill_received_pair
    CHECK ((status <> 'received') OR (received_at IS NOT NULL AND expiry_date IS NOT NULL))
)
```

⛔ **`spot_fill_received_pair` is the constraint that matters** and it is written to be **non-vacuous**:
it forbids a `received` row with a NULL expiry, which is the exact hole through which undated stock
would otherwise reach a shelf. ⭐ Per **S-177**, fixture 26 proves it by attempting the forbidden
`UPDATE` and requiring the raise — never by reading the predicate. ⛔ And note the shape trap S-177
punished: a `CHECK` whose predicate can evaluate to `NULL` **accepts** the row. This one cannot —
`status <> 'received'` is never NULL because `status` is `NOT NULL`.

⭐ `expiry_date` is deliberately **NULL-until-phase-2** rather than defaulted. ⛔ Never default it to
`2099-12-31`: that is the sentinel shape (P0.4) and it would make spot goods immortal in FEFO.

## 6. TRANSACTION ORDER

### Phase 1 (`receive_dispatch_line_sourced_v3`)

1. Validate: dispatch exists and `item_added = false` · `n >= 0` · `m >= 0` · `n + m > 0` · when
   `m > 0`, supplier is **Active** and `procurement_type = 'walk_in'`.
2. **WH leg — call the incumbent `receive_dispatch_line(p_dispatch_id, n, …)` unchanged.** It debits
   exactly n, so `v_overfill = GREATEST(n - planned, 0)` and the line-112 guard is never provoked by
   the spot units. ⭐ **This is the whole fix in one sentence:** the guard did not need changing, it
   needed to stop being asked a question about units that were never its business.
3. `m = 0` ⇒ return here. P4.4b is then exactly the incumbent, which is the common case.
4. **Spot leg**, `m > 0`: insert `spot_fill_v3` (`status='pending'`, expiry NULL) · resolve/attach the
   walk-in PO via `_resolve_open_walkin_po_v3` and add the PO line at qty m, **unreceived** ·
   increment shelf composition by m · `UPDATE refill_dispatching SET filled_quantity = n + m` with
   provenance set and an audited `app.mutation_reason`.
5. Emit `inventory_events` `kind='spot_buy_receive'`, `qty_delta = +m`. ⛔ Verified live: the kind
   CHECK admits `spot_buy_receive` and a **second** CHECK forces `qty_delta > 0` for it. ⭐ This
   matters beyond bookkeeping — cron 44's estimator reads this table, and **without the event the
   count rise would be mis-read as a `venue_fill` or flagged as an anomaly** (the fixture-19 path).
6. `p_dry_run` ⇒ everything inside a subtransaction, RAISE to roll back, return the would-be result.
   ⭐ Per **S-133**, say in the payload that DEFERRED constraints were not checked.

### Phase 2 (`receive_spot_fill_po_v3`)

1. Stamp `purchase_orders`: `received_date`, `received_qty`, `purchase_outcome='received'`,
   `total_price_aed`, `expiry_date` from the receipt.
2. ⛔ **No `warehouse_inventory` write. D-E.**
3. `spot_fill_v3` → `status='received'`, `received_at`, `expiry_date`; propagate the receipt expiry
   onto the shelf batch so FEFO and the expiry engine see real dates.
4. ⛔ **Composition is NOT touched.** Phase 1 already put the units on the shelf.

⛔ **THE HIGHEST-RISK DEFECT IN THE WHOLE UNIT IS A PHASE-2 DOUBLE-ADD** — closing the financial chain
must not re-add m to the shelf. Fixture 26 runs phase 2 **twice** and asserts composition is identical
after the second run (§7, seq 34-37). Idempotency here is not a nicety; it is the difference between
this feature and a stock-inflation bug.

- **Split integrity (17-24):** WH debited **exactly n**, never n+m (the phantom-mint assertion — it

- **Pod-level fallback (25-29):** `p_spot_breakdown = NULL` ⇒ one row at the line's own product,
  `sku_resolved = false`; a supplied breakdown ⇒ rows per SKU summing to m, `sku_resolved = true`.
- **D-E, pinned deliberately (30-33):** **zero** `warehouse_inventory` rows created by the spot leg ·

- **Phase-2 idempotency (34-37):** run twice ⇒ composition identical, `received_qty` identical, no
  second PO stamp.
- **Refusals, each proven to fire (38-43):** `received` with NULL expiry refused
  (`spot_fill_received_pair`) · `m > 0` with no supplier refused · Inactive supplier refused ·

- **Residue (44):** dry run leaves zero across all four protected tables — deltas reclaimed by the
  fixture's own marker, ⛔ never absolute counts (S-136/S-134).

⭐ Run it **twice, committed**, before calling it green (**S-136**); reconcile `n_fail` against failing
seqs plus `detail -> scenario_error` (**S-135**).

⚠️ **Conservation cannot be delegated to the nightly monitor, and this is a live finding.**
`check_pod_conservation(p_plan_date)` compares `pod_refill_plan` parents against `refill_dispatching`
children **for `action IN ('REMOVE','M2W')` only** (read this leg). It is structurally blind to a
Refill/Add post-facto spot fill. ⭐ Good news — cron 33 will **not** raise a spurious nightly critical
on P4.4b. ⛔ Bad news — "conservation exact end-to-end" is fixture 26's own burden, seq 17-24.

## 8. CODY REVIEW — verdict and conditions

**VERDICT: CONSTITUTIONAL, subject to the six conditions below.** Touches three protected entities
(`refill_dispatching`, `pod_inventory`, `purchase_orders`) and adds two SECURITY DEFINER functions.

1. **Article 3 — versioned additions only.** ✅ Two `_v3` additions that **compose** the incumbents.
   ⛔ The verdict is void the moment a leg finds it easier to edit `receive_dispatch_line` or to add a
   `skip_receive` flag to `create_spot_purchase_v3` (§3).
2. **Article 6 — `warehouse_inventory.status` manager-only.** ✅ **This is the constitutional core of
   D-E**: the rejected netting design writes that column on every fill, as executed and measured in
   §1. Direct-to-machine never touches `warehouse_inventory` at all.
3. **Article 4 — provenance.** ⛔ Set `app.via_rpc`/`app.rpc_name` immediately before each protected
   write and **RESTORE** at the end (S-160). Fixture 26 asserts the restore.
4. **Article 7 — append-only.** ✅ No DELETE. `spot_fill_v3` cancellation is `status='void'`, never a
   row removal.
5. **LAW 5 — no silent qty-0.** ✅ A spot fill the driver cannot complete (no supplier, bad qty)
   raises with a _guided_ message naming the missing field. ⛔ It must never fall back to recording
   only n and dropping m on the floor — that would lose 8 real units silently, which is the same
   class of harm as the original strand, minus the error message.
6. **LAW 12 — never touch live plan tables.** ⛔ Fixture 26 runs on a **2030** plan_date and a
   synthetic machine. ⚠️ **S-124 applies directly:** fixture residue has previously landed on the live
   protected plan table; `refill_dispatching` is protected and this fixture writes to it.

⏸️ **Cody's recorded condition.** P4.4b creates the first inventory in this system's history that is
**on a shelf while financially unreceived**. Every report that reconciles stock value against received
POs will now show a legitimate, transient gap. ⛔ Do not let a later leg "fix" that gap by
back-dating a warehouse receipt — that resurrects the netting design D-E rejected, and it would do so
in a place where nobody is looking. The gap is the honest representation of a real business state and
it closes on its own at phase 2.
